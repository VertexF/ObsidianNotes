# What are billboards

So there 3 types of billboards spherical, cylindrical and polyboards this is nothing to do with the geometry but how it's oritentation works relative to the camera. Polyboards are basically triangle trips that act like billboards

Polyboard are used for effects were billboards need to be connected to each other. Think of lightening effects and other such line graphical effects.

Polygon trimming is to do with optimisations for billboards with large transparent regions, useful for as tree impostors. This uses maths trim the corners off things.
