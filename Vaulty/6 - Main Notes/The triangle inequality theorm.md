# The triangle inequality theorm

This is for 1 side of the a triangle, it states 1 side can't be smaller than the difference of the 2 side, and can't be larger than the 2 sides added together.

![[basicTriangleOfX.png]]

So in this example the difference between the two side is $8.5 - 8 = 0.5$ and the sides added together $8+8.5 = 16.5$ so the for $x$ this most always be true $0.5 < x < 16.5$ 

Just because I got it wrong in the past, we are only ever allowed to resize length $x$ not the other 2.
