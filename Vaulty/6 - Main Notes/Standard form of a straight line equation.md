# Standard form of a straight line equation

This is simply $ax+by=c$ what to realise here is that the straight line equation tells you the $x$ intercept and the $y$ intercept very quickly. For example:
$4x+3y=12$
You can just set the $x$ value to $0$ and find that $3y=12$ making $y=4$ meaning that we have point $(0, 4)$ you can easily do the same for $y$ and the $x$ intercept with $(3, 0)$
