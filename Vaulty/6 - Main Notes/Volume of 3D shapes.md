# Volume of 3D shapes

The volume of a cone is 
$$\frac{h}{3}\pi r^2 = V$$
![[cone Volume.png]]

The volume of a cone is
$$\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 = V$$
![[sphere volume.png]]

The volume of a cyclinder is 
$$hr^2\pi = V$$
